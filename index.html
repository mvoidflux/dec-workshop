ABOUT THE WORKSHOP

Learn autonomous systems by building them.

This three-day workshop is designed to provide M.Tech and final-year B.Tech students with practical experience in building autonomous systems.

The workshop combines limited classroom lectures with extensive hands-on laboratory sessions. Participants will work with microcontrollers, sensors, actuators and communication systems before progressing toward complete autonomous systems.

The workshop culminates in the construction and testing of a self-balancing two-wheel robot and the development of a smart home energy management system.
