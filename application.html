Application Instructions

Fill out the Google Form linked at the bottom of this page.

Due to the limited number of seats, a two-step process will be followed. Applicants will be shortlisted based on the criteria described below. Shortlisted applicants will be invited to pay the registration fee.

Applicants who complete the payment within the specified time will have their participation confirmed. Any remaining seats will be offered to the next set of applicants, and the process will continue accordingly.

Documents Required Before Applying

  • A scanned copy of a government-issued ID.
  • Class XII school-leaving examination marksheet.
  • B.Tech degree certificate, if you have completed your B.Tech. Otherwise, upload your grade transcript up to and including the 6th semester.
  • M.Tech degree certificate, if you have completed your M.Tech. Otherwise, upload your grade transcript up to and including the 2nd semester of your M.Tech programme.

What Happens After You Apply?

After submitting the application form, you will receive an acknowledgement. If you are shortlisted for participation, you will receive an email by November 5, 2026, inviting you to pay the registration fee.

Once your payment is completed, an email confirming your seat in the workshop will be sent to you.
