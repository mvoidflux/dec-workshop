PROGRAM

Three days of hands-on learning.

DAY 01

Embedded Systems & Hardware

Introduction to autonomous systems, embedded-C programming, microcontrollers, sensors, actuators and communication.

DAY 02

Sensing, Communication & Control

Guided laboratory work involving sensors, GPS, communication systems, power systems and feedback control.

DAY 03

Autonomous Systems & Capstone Projects

Build and test autonomous systems including a self-balancing two-wheel robot and a smart home energy management system.

02

ABOUT THE WORKSHOP

Learn autonomous systems by building them.

This three-day workshop is designed to provide M.Tech students and final-year B.Tech students/graduates with practical experience in building autonomous systems.
