Application Instructions

Due to limited number of seats, a two step process is followed. Applicants will be shortlisted based on criteria below. Those who are shortlisted will be invited to pay the registration fee. Those who complete the registration fee payment on time will be confirmed for participation. Any seats remaining will be offered to the next set of applicants. And so on.

Documents you need before filling out the application form

  • Scan of a government-issued ID
  • Class XII school leaving exam marksheet.
  • B.Tech degree certificate, if you have a B.Tech degree. If not, grade transcipt upto and including 6-th semester.
  • M.Tech degree certificate. If not, grade transcipt upto and including 2-nd semester of your M.Tech program

After you apply:

After you submit the application form, you would receive an acknowledgement. If you are shortlisted for participation, you would receive an email by November 5, 2026, inviting you to pay the registration fee. Once you pay, a confirmation for your sear in the workshop will be sent by email.

Ready to Apply?

Ensure that you have the required academic documents ready before proceeding to the application form.
