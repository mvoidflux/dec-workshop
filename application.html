STEP 01

Before You Apply

Before starting your application, please review the workshop information available on the main website.

  • Read the workshop overview and program schedule.
  • Review the workshop topics and instructor information.
  • Check the registration fee before proceeding.
  • Keep your personal and academic information ready for completing the application form.
STEP 02

Complete the Application

  1. Click the application button at the bottom of this page.
  2. Enter the information requested in the registration form.
  3. Review all the information you have entered before submitting the form.
  4. Submit the completed form and follow any instructions displayed after submission.
STEP 03

Registration Fee

Registration Fee ₹2,000 + GST

Please follow the official payment instructions provided by the workshop organizers or in the registration form.

PLEASE NOTE

Important Information

  • Ensure that all information submitted is accurate and complete.
  • Follow the instructions provided in the official registration form.
  • Keep a record of your application submission for future reference.
  • Refer to official communications from the workshop organizers for further updates.
