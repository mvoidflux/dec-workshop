Application Instructions

Hands-on Workshop on Autonomous Systems

Please read the application procedure, screening criteria, document requirements, and registration instructions carefully before applying.

IMPORTANT DATES

Application & Registration Deadlines

Application Deadline 30 October 2026
Registration Completion Deadline 10 November 2026
STEP 01

How to Apply?

Applicants can apply for the workshop by completing the Google Form provided below.

  1. Click the Proceed to Application button at the bottom of this page.
  2. Fill in the required personal, academic, and contact information.
  3. Upload the required academic documents according to your degree status.
  4. Enter your GATE score, if applicable.
  5. Review your responses and submit the form before 30 October 2026.
STEP 02

Required Documents

Applicants must upload the relevant academic documents based on their current degree and completion status.

B.Tech Applicants

  • Currently pursuing B.Tech: Combined semester marksheets from the 1st to the 6th semester.
  • B.Tech graduates: Complete B.Tech transcript.

M.Tech Applicants

  • Currently pursuing M.Tech: Semester marksheets up to the 2nd semester.
  • M.Tech graduates: Complete M.Tech transcript.
STEP 03

How Will Applicants Be Screened?

Applications will be evaluated based on the academic and examination details provided in the application form.

The screening process will consider the following factors:

  • CGPA: Academic performance in the applicant's current or completed degree.
  • GATE Score: GATE score, if applicable.
  • Degree: The applicant's degree, including B.Tech or M.Tech, and their current or completed academic status.
STEP 04

Registration & Seat Confirmation

Filling out the Google Form is the first step of the application process. Registration will be completed through the following procedure:

  1. Submit the application: Complete and submit the Google Form with the required information and documents.
  2. Application screening: The submitted application will be reviewed based on the specified screening criteria.
  3. Receive the payment link: The payment link will be emailed to applicants after the form submission and subsequent screening process.
  4. Complete payment: Follow the instructions provided in the email to pay the registration fee.
  5. Confirm your seat: Your seat will be confirmed after the required payment has been completed.

Please check your registered email address regularly for the payment link and further instructions from the workshop organizers.

Ready to Apply?

Ensure that you have the required academic documents ready before proceeding to the application form.
